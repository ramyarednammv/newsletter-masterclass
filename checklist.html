This week's homework

This week — four things before Thursday

Stuck on something in Kit?

Ask Kit directly — don't sit on it for a week. Their support is good, and answering setup questions is exactly what they're there for. It's almost always faster than waiting for Thursday.

  • Use the help or support link inside your Kit dashboard — quickest route, and it already knows which account you're on
  • Search their help centre first. Most setup questions are already answered, with screenshots
  • When you ask, say exactly what you clicked and exactly what happened. "I clicked send test and nothing arrived" gets a useful answer. "It's not working" doesn't
  • Then drop it in the group as well — if you hit it, someone else has too
